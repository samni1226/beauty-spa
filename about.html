Our Story

Built by two friends who love pink & pampering

Since 2018

From a spare bedroom to a full studio

Bloom & Glow started in 2018 when co-founders Riya and Sana began giving facials out of a spare bedroom, mixing rose-clay masks by hand for friends who wanted a gentler, unhurried kind of pampering.

Seven years later, that same care shapes every corner of our studio — soft pink walls, calming playlists, and a team that remembers your favorite scent of candle. We grew slowly, on word of mouth, because we never wanted to rush a single appointment.
